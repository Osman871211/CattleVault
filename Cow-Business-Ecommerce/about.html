Our Mission

Transforming Livestock Trading with Digital Transparency

CattleVault was initiated as an innovative Diploma Engineering Final Year Project designed to eliminate middleman exploitation in agricultural trade. Our mission is to empower dairy farmers and livestock breeders with a digital marketplace featuring certified health records, verified weights, and secure logistics.
